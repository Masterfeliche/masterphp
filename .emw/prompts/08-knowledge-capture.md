# EMW Prompt 08 — Knowledge Capture

Use after discovering a durable, non-obvious lesson, especially after debugging or completing a meaningful integration, architectural change, security review, or learning milestone.

1. Ask whether the discovery is reusable and likely to prevent repeated work.
2. Separate verified facts, hypotheses, opinions, and context-specific assumptions.
3. Gather provenance: relevant files, commit, tests, command output, issue, ADR, authoritative documentation, or reproducible steps.
4. Write the problem, context, root cause or principle, solution, alternatives, evidence, limitations, affected versions, and related records.
5. Choose project-local scope by default. Use `.project-knowledge/`.
6. Check for duplicates, contradictions, sensitive data, credentials, personal information, private code, and copied copyrighted material.
7. Assign draft/proposed/verified status and low/medium/high confidence honestly.
8. Update the appropriate index.
9. Do not promote project-specific knowledge into shared EMW automatically. Use Prompt 10 for a human-reviewed contribution.
10. Keep the entry concise enough to retrieve and detailed enough to apply safely.

Do not capture routine edits, unverified guesses as facts, secrets, customer data, or noisy conversation transcripts. If evidence is insufficient, save as a clearly labelled draft or do not save it.
